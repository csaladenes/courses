An Agent is an autonomous entity that:

  • Perceives its local environment (e.g., "Is there a sick neighbor nearby?").

Math Deep Dive: Lotka-Volterra

The continuous change in populations is described by these differential equations:

$$ \frac{dx}{dt} = \alpha x - \beta xy $$ $$ \frac{dy}{dt} = \delta xy - \gamma y $$

\(x\) = Prey (Rabbits), \(y\) = Predator (Foxes).
Notice the Coupling Term \(xy\): Interaction depends on both populations meeting.

6. The Meta-Link: Agentic AI

Connecting System Dynamics, Agent-Based Modeling, and the future of AI Agents.

From Rules to Prompts

In our SIR and Predator simulations, agents followed simple code rules: if (rabbit_near) eat();.

Agentic AI replaces these hard-coded rules with LLM Prompts. An autonomous AI agent is a "Game Theory Player" navigating a "Network Topology" of APIs and humans.

The New "Code"

  • Old ABM: Fixed rules, predictable interactions.
  • New Agentic AI: Natural language goals, emergent strategies, hallucination risks.

System Dynamics 2.0

Just like in People Express, where hiring too fast crashed the system, deploying thousands of AI agents creates new system dynamics.

When millions of trading bots (Agents) interact in the stock market, we get "Flash Crashes" (Emergence). Understanding Feedback Loops and Network Topology is critical for AI Safety.
