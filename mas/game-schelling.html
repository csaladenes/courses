Micromotives & Macrobehavior

Nobel laureate Thomas Schelling showed that extreme segregation can emerge even if individuals are not extreme.

If people prefer just "30% similarity" (a very tolerant rule!), the system still self-organizes into total separation (80%+ similarity).

Tipping Points

Try setting Tolerance to 70% vs 75%. Small changes in individual rules can cause phase transitions in the global state.
