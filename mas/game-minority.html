The Concept

Based on the El Farol Bar Problem (Brian Arthur, 1994). If everyone goes to the bar, it's crowded and no one has fun. If no one goes, it's empty and boring.

The goal is to be in the Minority. This models resource competition where resources are scarce (e.g., road capacity, stock market arbitrage).

Why it Oscillates

If "Option A" wins round 1, agents adapt and pick "Option A" next time... which makes it crowded, causing "Option B" to win.

This creates Volatility. A perfectly efficient market would stay at a 50/50 split, but human agents using heuristics (past history) create constant fluctuations.
